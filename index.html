Product teardown · villa-tax.operium.store · Nov 2026

One booking becomes a tax obligation.

VillaTax is an operating system for Indonesian villas, mostly in Bali. It pulls bookings from Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Traveloka and other channels and turns each one into revenue, ledger entries and tax lines (PBJT, PPh, PPN). It then schedules the filings for DJP Coretax, the regency tax office and OSS. It prepares filings but does not submit them. A human, either you or PT Asiah Legal Jaya, does the filing.

03 · Third parties

Who VillaTax talks to, and how

VillaTax does not replace Airbnb, Traveloka or a PMS. It sits behind them. Booking channels push data in, mostly through iCal and CSV files. Government systems only receive data through files that a human uploads. Click a card for details.

How the tax engine decides

05 · Business model

Free tools in, software, then experts

The software is the hook and the experts are the margin. Free calculators and diagnostics bring owners in. Subscriptions are priced per organization, not per villa. Higher-value revenue comes from Shield retainers and fixed-fee missions delivered by PT Asiah Legal Jaya, the Bali company that operates VillaTax and issues its invoices.

06 · Findings

What stood out while clicking through

The three findings that matter most, each with a screenshot from the read-only demo workspace (Seminyak Villas SAS). Red boxes mark the evidence. Click a screenshot to enlarge it.

1 Conflicting claims Nothing is filed to Coretax automatically

The in-app Coretax boundary screen says no tax obligation is sent to DJP automatically today. Of 12 obligations mapped, 0 can be automated, because the PJAP channel is closed and every filing needs human review.

The public FAQ says the opposite: “select declarations can be submitted directly through the platform”. Trust the product screen.

Tax & compliance → Tax scope
2 Assumption OTA bookings look like iCal and CSV, not live webhooks

In the demo, Airbnb and Booking.com are marked Webhook, yet both show last sync “never” and 0 bookings imported. The only inputs on each card are an iCal URL and CSV import. The connector catalogue lists two methods: iCal sync (46 platforms) and manual setup (264).

This is an assumption. From what the demo shows, bookings do not arrive in real time. VillaTax may still have a real partnership with Airbnb or Booking.com that is switched off in demo mode or enabled per account. The “Webhook” label and the FAQ's mention of OAuth connections point that way. Confirm with VillaTax before relying on either reading.

If the connections really are iCal and CSV only, then iCal carries only dates. Revenue and every tax line would depend on CSV imports or manual entry.

Operations → Integrations
3 Bug Occupancy shows 112%

The Analytics header shows occupancy at 112%, which is impossible. The likely cause is that booked nights are counted across overlapping stays or divided by the wrong number of available nights.

Gross revenue also differs between screens: Rp 6.64B here and Rp 7.38B on Stays. They probably use different default periods, but nothing on the page says so.
